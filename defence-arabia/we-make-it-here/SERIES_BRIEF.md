==================================================
CRITICAL LOCALIZATION TAXONOMY
==================================================

Never use the word "LOCAL" or "INDIGENOUS" without defining what it means.

Classify every programme as one or more of:
1. INDIGENOUS DESIGN — Designed domestically.
2. JOINT DEVELOPMENT — Designed with foreign partner participation.
3. LICENSED PRODUCTION — Foreign design manufactured under licence.
4. LOCAL ASSEMBLY — Major imported components assembled locally.
5. COMPONENT LOCALIZATION — Some components produced domestically.
6. MRO — Maintenance, repair and overhaul only.
7. TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER — Documented transfer of specific technology / know-how.
8. JOINT VENTURE — Industrial partnership with shared corporate structure.
9. LOCAL INTEGRATION — Foreign subsystems integrated locally.
10. DOMESTIC BRAND / FOREIGN CORE TECHNOLOGY — Locally branded system using substantial imported technology.

Do not collapse these categories into "locally made."

==================================================
LOCAL CONTENT PERCENTAGE RULE
==================================================

Never invent "70% local", "100% national", "fully indigenous" unless a credible source explicitly provides the figure.

When a percentage exists, explain WHAT IS BEING MEASURED: value share? parts count? labour? production? subsystems? contract spending?

Never compare two localization percentages unless they measure the same thing.

==================================================
TECHNOLOGY TRANSFER RULE
==================================================

"Technology transfer" is frequently overused. Require evidence.
Investigate exactly what transferred: manufacturing process? assembly know-how? maintenance? software? source code? design authority? materials technology? munition production? testing capability? training?
Do not call ordinary operator training technology transfer.

==================================================
MOU RULE
==================================================

MoU ≠ factory. MoU ≠ production. MoU ≠ technology transfer. MoU ≠ contract.
Always distinguish: MoU, framework agreement, joint venture agreement, licensing agreement, production contract, factory opening, actual serial production.

==================================================
INDUSTRIAL SUCCESS RULE
==================================================

Measurable outcomes: factory established? production started? deliveries made? local workforce trained? domestic suppliers involved? follow-on production? exports? second-generation local product? local upgrades? maintenance capability?
Do not use ceremony photos as proof of industrial depth.

==================================================
ARAB PRIDE WITHOUT PROPAGANDA
==================================================

The series may celebrate real Arab industrial achievements. Strong framing is allowed when evidence supports it: "From importer to producer", "Designed in the UAE", "Built in Saudi Arabia", "Egypt expands local military production".
Never inflate: assembly → indigenous design; JV → full ownership; planned factory → operational production.
Credibility makes achievements more impressive.
