SAR 56 Rifle: Sarsılmaz's 5.56 mm Infantry Rifle Explained

The SAR 56 is a 5.56×45 mm NATO infantry rifle family made by the Turkish small-arms manufacturer Sarsılmaz. It uses a short-stroke gas piston system and is offered with three barrel lengths: 7.5, 11 and 14.5 inches. According to Sarsılmaz's product data, the 14.5-inch model is 880 mm long, weighs 3,490 g without a magazine, fires at 750–950 rounds per minute and has a stated effective range of 500 m. Sarsılmaz says it developed the rifle with the requirements of the Turkish Armed Forces, the Gendarmerie and the national police in mind.

What is the SAR 56?

The SAR 56 is Sarsılmaz's own design of select-fire 5.56 mm rifle. In its product descriptions, the company says the series builds on its experience with the earlier SAR 223 rifles and with series production of the MPT-76, and that feedback from field personnel shaped the final design. The MPT-76 is the 7.62×51 mm national infantry rifle designed by MKE; in February 2017 Hürriyet Daily News, quoting Turkey's defence procurement agency, reported that Sarsılmaz would manufacture some 10,000 MPT-76 rifles for the Turkish land, naval and gendarmerie forces.

A Sarsılmaz article on the rifle says the project started with the aim of meeting the requirements of the Turkish Armed Forces, the Gendarmerie General Command and the Security General Directorate (police), and describes the result as a rifle capable of "at least 50,000 shots". The company does not say in that text whether this refers to barrel life or total service life.

Design features

Sarsılmaz lists the following features across the SAR 56 series:

  • Short-stroke gas piston operation in 5.56×45 mm.
  • Safe, semi-automatic and fully automatic fire modes.
  • Five-position buttstock with cheek support, and a supported handguard.
  • A latch group that can be operated from both sides, and the ability to put the rifle on safe when it is not cocked.
  • Polymer dust cover, sling attachment loop and a magazine with a round-count indicator.
  • Six-groove, right-hand rifling with a 1:7-inch (178 mm) twist.
  • Magazines of 30, 20 or 10 rounds.

The piston layout is the same basic approach used by MKE's MPT-55 and by Heckler & Koch's HK416, in which gas from the barrel drives a short piston rather than being piped into the bolt carrier as in the classic AR-15.

SAR 56C SA, SAR 56 HMT and SAR 39

SAR 56C SA

Sarsılmaz lists a separate SAR 56C SA model. Its product page shows the same core figures as the 14.5-inch rifle (369 mm barrel, 880 mm length, 3,490 g) and the same three fire modes. The company does not explain the suffix on that page, so we do not describe the model further here.

SAR 56 HMT light machine gun

The SAR 56 HMT is a light machine gun derived from the same family. Its datasheet (revision 2, January 2025) gives a 368.3 mm (14.5-inch) quick-change barrel with a carrying handle, full-automatic fire only, feed from a 30-round magazine or M27 link belt, a rate of about 600–1,000 rounds per minute, a length of 880 mm with the stock extended and 825 mm collapsed, and a maximum weight of 4,500 g.

SAR 39

At IDEF 2025, Sarsılmaz presented the SAR 39, which it describes as a new infantry rifle in 7.62×39 mm developed from the SAR 56 platform, with 11-inch and 14.5-inch barrel options.

Who uses the SAR 56?

Sarsılmaz states that the SAR 56 was designed around the needs of the Turkish Armed Forces, the Gendarmerie and the police. We have not found a public procurement announcement or delivery report that confirms SAR 56 orders, quantities or in-service status, so this page does not list confirmed users. Sarsılmaz is an established supplier to the same institutions in other product lines; the SAR9 METE CE pistol, for example, was selected as a sidearm for TSK officers and NCOs. FAQ

What calibre is the SAR 56?

5.56×45 mm NATO. A 7.62×39 mm derivative, the SAR 39, was shown at IDEF 2025.

Who makes the SAR 56?

The Turkish manufacturer Sarsılmaz, which also built MKE's MPT-76 rifle under a 2017 contract.

What is the effective range of the SAR 56?

Sarsılmaz gives 500 m for the 14.5-inch model, 400 m for the 11-inch model and 300 m for the 7.5-inch model.

How much does the SAR 56 weigh?

From 3,050 g (7.5-inch) to 3,490 g (14.5-inch), without magazine, according to Sarsılmaz.

Is the SAR 56 the same as the MPT-55?

No. Both are Turkish 5.56 mm short-stroke piston rifles, but the SAR 56 is a Sarsılmaz design and the MPT-55 is made by MKE.

Is the SAR 56 in Turkish military service?

Sarsılmaz says it was developed for Turkish Armed Forces, Gendarmerie and police requirements. We could not find an official procurement or delivery announcement confirming service use.
