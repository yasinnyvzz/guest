ASELSAN Signs $1.1 Billion Air Defence Export Contract With an Undisclosed Customer

ASELSAN has signed a US$1.1 billion export contract with an international customer for the sale of air defence systems, the Turkish defence electronics company said in a filing to Türkiye's Public Disclosure Platform (KAP) published at 10:07 on 3 November 2026. The "New Business Relation" disclosure does not name the customer, the country or the systems, and gives no quantities or delivery schedule. It says only that the contract will have a positive impact on company revenues.

What the KAP filing says

The explanatory section of the disclosure is a single sentence: "ASELSAN has signed an export contract with an international customer, valued at USD 1.1 billion, for the sale of Air Defense Systems." The filing notes that the English text is a translation and that the Turkish version prevails. The structured fields of the form add little:

The 0.0% shares mean the customer did not account for a measurable part of ASELSAN's sales or receivables in its latest published financial statements. The filing does not explain this further.

What remains unknown

ASELSAN's air defence portfolio covers several gun, missile, radar and command-and-control products, but the disclosure does not say which of them, or which combination, the contract covers. Envanter is not naming a system or a buyer, because neither has been officially disclosed. Our Turkish-language report on the same filing takes the same approach.

How the deal compares

According to Türkiye Today, ASELSAN's new export contracts in the first nine months of 2026 rose 34% to $1.9 billion, total new orders reached $14 billion and the order backlog passed $30 billion for the first time. On those figures, the single $1.1 billion contract is equal to more than half of the company's nine-month export bookings. Envanter covered the nine-month results and backlog in detail.

The filing follows a run of disclosures in recent weeks. Türkiye Today notes that export contracts announced in October covered radar, counter-drone, command-and-control and other systems, among them a $442.5 million radar and counter-UAS export contract and a $157.9 million deal spanning electronic warfare and weapon systems. On the domestic side, ASELSAN has signed air defence contracts with Türkiye's Presidency of Defence Industries (SSB) for long-range and hybrid systems, including a €2.46 billion hybrid air defence contract; Envanter has tallied six 2026 air defence contracts worth about €7.48 billion.

Company comment

ASELSAN has not commented on the customer beyond the filing. Speaking about the company's growth, General Manager Ahmet Akyol said, as quoted by Türkiye Today: "We do not wait for orders to outpace our capacity. We turn orders into capacity, and capacity into the future." Türkiye Today also reported that revenue rose 28% to ₺151.5 billion in January–September and that research and development spending increased 36% to $1.3 billion.
