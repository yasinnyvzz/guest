## 0. Why the original topic pool was changed

The 2026 US–Israel war on Iran changed which topics readers are looking for:

- **Air attacks on the Gulf:** from 28 Feb 2026 Iran fired missiles and drones at all six GCC states and Jordan.
- **Hormuz:** closure, US blockade and a mine campaign.
- **Energy and water:** Ras Tanura, Ras Laffan, Kuwaiti desalination and power plants were hit.
- **Red Sea:** Houthi attacks on shipping resumed on 22 Jul 2026.
- **Electronic warfare:** large-scale GPS jamming.

Semrush returned no data (the account has no API units left), so demand was judged from how heavily Arabic media covered each topic in 2026:

- **مخزون الصواريخ الاعتراضية** (interceptor stockpiles): heavy coverage by Al Jazeera, Annahar and Arabi21.
- **تحلية المياه** (desalination): heavy coverage.
- **GPS jamming:** covered.
- **كلفة اعتراض المسيّرات** (cost of intercepting drones): covered by Al Majalla and Al Khaleej.

## 3. Editorial rules (apply to every episode)

1. **Safety boundary.** Never include:
   - attack planning
   - exact locations or active air-defence positions
   - radar blind spots or approach routes
   - interceptor depletion or saturation arithmetic
   - "how to defeat system X" content
   - EW bypass methods
   - target selection
   - convoy routes, mine-clearing procedures or chokepoint tactics
2. **No national winner.** Use indicator matrices only (confirmed interceptors, layers, AEW&C, radars, C-UAS, pipeline, training, C2, recent investments).
3. **Evidence labels:** confirmed / contracted / approved (FMS ≠ contract) / reported / unverified.
4. **Turkish system rule.** Always CHECK. INCLUDE only with the same evidence standard as competitors. Never invent an advantage.
5. **Envanter Media.** Maximum 1 backlink per article, only when relevant. The domain is `envantermedya.com`.
6. **Cost asymmetry.** Use public unit-cost categories only, framed as procurement economics.
7. **Maps.** Broad regional maps only: countries, sea lanes, chokepoints, operator icons. No positions.
8. **Evergreen URLs.** No year in the slug. Update the title, H1 and data annually.
