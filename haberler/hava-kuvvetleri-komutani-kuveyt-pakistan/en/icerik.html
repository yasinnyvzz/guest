The commander of the Turkish Air Force is hosting the commander of the Kuwait Air Force in Türkiye on 11-14 November 2026. He is then scheduled to pay an official visit to Pakistan on 16-19 November. The Ministry of National Defence (MSB) gave the schedule at its weekly press briefing on 12 November.

Two engagements in one week

  • 11-14 November: Kuwait Air Force commander's visit to Türkiye
  • 16-19 November: Turkish Air Force commander's official visit to Pakistan

How it appeared in the briefing

Both items were listed in the section where the ministry runs through senior commanders' activities. The Kuwaiti commander's visit was under way at the time of the briefing; the Pakistan trip was announced as a planned activity.

What was not disclosed

The ministry did not say which units and facilities the Kuwaiti delegation is visiting, what is on the agenda, whom the Turkish commander will meet in Pakistan or whether any agreement will be signed.

Source: Turkish Ministry of National Defence weekly press briefing, 12 November 2026.
