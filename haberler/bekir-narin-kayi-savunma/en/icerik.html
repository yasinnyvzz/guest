According to his own LinkedIn profile, Bekir Narin has been General Manager (CEO) of Ankara-based defence company KAYI SAVUNMA since August 2026. He previously served as a director at Roketsan and as a group coordinator at TÜBİTAK, and holds bachelor's, master's and doctoral degrees in aerospace engineering from Middle East Technical University (METU).
