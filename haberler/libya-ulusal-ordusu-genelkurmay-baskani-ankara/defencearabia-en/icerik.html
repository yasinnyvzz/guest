Türkiye's defence ministry has listed back-to-back contacts between the Turkish Chief of the General Staff and the leadership of the Libyan National Army (LNA). The sequence, as given on 12 November 2026:

11 November

The Turkish Chief of the General Staff holds a telephone conversation with the LNA's Deputy Commander-in-Chief.

12 November

The LNA's chief of staff and a delegation, in Türkiye by official invitation, are received by the Turkish Chief of the General Staff.

Context

The LNA controls eastern Libya. The ministry presented both items in its routine weekly list of senior officers' engagements, without commentary.

Not stated

Who is in the delegation, the duration of the stay, the topics, and whether any document is to be signed.

Source: Turkish Ministry of National Defence weekly briefing, 12 November 2026.
