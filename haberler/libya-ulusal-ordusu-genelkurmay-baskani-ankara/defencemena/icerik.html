Two military contacts in two days link Ankara and eastern Libya this week: a phone call on Wednesday, followed on Thursday, 12 November 2026, by a delegation headed by the Libyan National Army's chief of staff.

How it unfolded

Türkiye's defence ministry told its weekly briefing that the country's top general phoned the LNA's Deputy Commander-in-Chief on 11 November. The next day his diary showed him receiving the LNA chief of staff and accompanying officers, who came on an official invitation.

Reading the timing

The LNA is headquartered in Libya's east. A call and a visit at this rank inside 48 hours point to an active military channel between the two sides.

Missing details

The ministry named no members of the delegation and said nothing about how long they will stay, what is on the table or whether agreements are expected.

Source: Turkish Ministry of National Defence weekly briefing, 12 November 2026.
