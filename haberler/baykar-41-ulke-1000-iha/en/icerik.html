Baykar announced in a presentation held as part of ITU Future Engineers Summit 2026 that it has delivered more than 1,000 unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) to 41 different countries.

Key figures from the presentation

  • 41 countries: the number of countries to which Baykar systems have been delivered.
  • More than 1,000 UAVs: total deliveries to these countries.

The event: Future Engineers Summit 2026

Organised by the ITU Career Center, Future Engineers Summit 2026 took place on 2-6 November 2026 on ITU's Maslak and Gümüşsuyu campuses. Engineering and technology managers, HR professionals and talent-acquisition teams from leading Turkish companies met ITU engineering candidates on campus. Baykar was the main sponsor of the previous edition in 2024.

Comparison with earlier figures

The figures on Baykar's corporate website reflect an earlier position: its "About" page lists 39 export countries and more than 1,000 delivered UAVs. The profile of Selçuk Bayraktar on the same site says that, as of November 2026, Baykar's original platforms have been exported to 40 countries. The newly announced 41 suggests the number is still rising. Baykar Chairman Selçuk Bayraktar also said recently that the company operates in more than 40 countries and has facilities in 7.

Country counts vary by source and date: countries with signed export contracts, countries with deliveries and countries of operation are not the same thing. The figure of 41 should therefore be read as the delivery count given in the presentation.

Why it matters

More than 1,000 deliveries and a 41-country customer base put concrete numbers on Baykar's export scale. That the figures were shared at an engineering summit, in a talk aimed at engineering candidates, also underlines the company's emphasis on careers and talent.

Sources: ITU Career Center announcement for Future Engineers Summit 2026; Baykar corporate website ("About" and Selçuk Bayraktar pages). A detailed breakdown of deliveries by platform was not disclosed.
