A remark by Algerian President Abdelmadjid Tebboune that Algeria is on brotherly terms with the Gulf states "except one country I will not name" is circulating again on social media. The remark itself is not new: Tebboune made it in October 2025 on a television programme after visiting the Ministry of Defence. It has regained attention since Algeria cut diplomatic relations with the UAE on 10 September 2026.

What did Tebboune say?

According to Algerian and Arab media, Tebboune described relations with Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Iraq, Qatar and Oman as brotherly and said the problem lay with those who interfere in Algeria's internal affairs. He was reported as saying Algeria does not allow interference even by great powers. Tebboune did not name the country. Algerian and Arab outlets read it as a reference to the UAE; that is the media's interpretation, not Tebboune's own statement.

What happened next?

According to France 24's report of 10 September 2026, Algeria severed diplomatic relations with the UAE in a decision carried by state media. The foreign ministry gave the UAE ambassador 48 hours to comply. It cited the "exhaustion of all means to preserve bilateral ties" and accused the UAE of "provocative and hostile acts", without giving details.

The same report quotes Tebboune from a televised interview in July: "Wherever they set foot, blood flows." He also said a diplomatic break "would have happened long ago" had Algeria not wanted to avoid deepening divisions in the Arab world. The report adds that Tebboune has often called the UAE a "micro-state", citing Libya, Mali and Sudan.

The UAE's response

Anwar Gargash, diplomatic adviser to the UAE president, wrote on X that relations should rest on "rationality, communication and clarity of interests" rather than "riddles and signals that require decoding". The UAE foreign ministry later said it hoped Algeria's decision would be temporary and that it remained committed to preserving ties between the two peoples and shared interests.

Earlier steps

The report also says Algeria began cancelling a 2013 air services agreement with the UAE in February. Algerian media have accused the UAE of sowing regional discord; the UAE has not published a detailed response to those accusations.

Why it matters

The rivalry in North Africa, the Sahel and Libya also plays out through defence procurement and security partnerships. The Algeria–UAE break is worth watching for the Gulf's reach in Africa and Algeria's alignment choices. But the remark resurfacing does not amount to a new statement; the old remark and the current rupture should be kept apart.
