Kenya does not publish what its military students earn. Trainees and cadets in the Kenya Defence Forces (KDF) draw pay during training, yet the Ministry of Defence has put no stipend figure in any public notice, and the numbers found online rest on unsourced salary lists.

Three figures, no official source

Secondary sites quote Ksh 7,172 a month for a trainee or recruit, Ksh 11,852 for a cadet, and Ksh 24,520 for an Officer Cadet with three years of service. Nairobi Law Monthly is one outlet that lists them. The sites do not agree on which figure belongs to which stage of training, and none cites a Ministry of Defence or Salaries and Remuneration Commission (SRC) document.

What the official record does show

The Ministry's recruitment material covers eligibility and training, not pay. The Regular GSO Cadet route runs three years toward a BSc in Defence and Security Studies. The Graduate route takes 15 months, and Specialist Officers train for six months. Our earlier guide, KDF Salary Scale, found no rank-by-rank pay table in the public domain either.

Why it matters now

The Ministry opened a December 2026 recruitment drive with a 12 December deadline, so many young applicants are asking what a place would pay. Unverified lists fill that gap. Calling letters and fees are a known scam pattern around each KDF intake, and the Ministry says recruitment is free.

Reading the numbers

Treat Ksh 7,172, Ksh 11,852 and Ksh 24,520 as unconfirmed estimates. They may reflect older pay bands, and allowances such as risk or housing are not documented for trainees at all. Applicants should rely on terms issued through official KDF channels.

Sources: Ministry of Defence (mod.go.ke); Nairobi Law Monthly; Defence MENA archive.
