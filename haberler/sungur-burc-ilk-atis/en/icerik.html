ROKETSAN's SUNGUR air defense missile destroyed a moving aerial target in its first firing from the BURÇ Mobile Air Defense System. The development was announced in a TRT Haber report dated 14 September 2026.

What happened in the firing?

The report describes the shot as SUNGUR's first launch from BURÇ and says the target was a moving aerial target. The location, date, target type, range and number of missiles fired were not shared.

Why does this firing stand out?

SUNGUR has mostly been known as a shoulder-fired (MANPADS) missile. Launching the same missile from a mobile air defense system suggests it is not limited to a single mode of use. For how the missile compares with the Stinger, see our Sungur vs Stinger comparison.

Not disclosed

  • BURÇ's inventory status and how many missiles it carries
  • The speed and altitude of the target used in the shot
  • The serial production schedule for the SUNGUR–BURÇ integrated configuration

Source: TRT Haber, 14 September 2026.
