A report that Türkiye would send 15,000 soldiers to Somalia was put to the Turkish defence ministry on 12 November 2026. Here is what was asked, what was answered and what remains unknown.

What was the claim?

That Mogadishu had asked for Turkish troops and that Ankara would respond with a force of 15,000.

What did the ministry answer?

That the claim is false. In the ministry's wording, it does "not reflect the truth". The answer was given to journalists at the weekly briefing, held this week on the TCG Savarona.

What do Turkish forces do in Somalia?

Two Turkish formations operate there, a joint Somali-Turkish Task Force and an Air Component Command. Their work is described as military aid, training and advisory support to help Somalia fight terrorism.

Under what mandate?

Türkiye's parliament voted on 21 July 2026 to prolong the armed forces' Somalia authorisation by two years, counted from 27 July 2026.

What is still unknown?

Current Turkish troop numbers in Somalia, any plan to enlarge the mission, and who first circulated the 15,000 figure.

Source: Turkish Ministry of National Defence weekly briefing, 12 November 2026.
