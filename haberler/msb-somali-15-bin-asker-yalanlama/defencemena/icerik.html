Is Türkiye preparing to put a 15,000-strong force into Somalia? Ankara's official answer is no. On Thursday, 12 November 2026, the Ministry of National Defence said reports to that effect are untrue.

The ministry's answer

Journalists raised the issue at the ministry's weekly media briefing, chaired by spokesperson Rear Admiral Zeki Aktürk and hosted this time on board the presidential yacht TCG Savarona. The response was that talk of Türkiye dispatching 15,000 soldiers "at Somalia's request" does "not reflect the truth".

Mission continues, no change announced

The ministry did not signal any drawdown either. It said Türkiye keeps two military formations in Somalia, the Somali-Turkish Task Force and an Air Component Command, working in three areas: military assistance, training and advice. The declared purpose is to raise Mogadishu's ability to counter terrorism.

The mandate

Turkish forces are in Somalia under a parliamentary authorisation that lawmakers renewed in July 2026 for a further two years, taking it to mid-2028.

Questions left open

The reply gave no figure for the Turkish personnel now in Somalia, did not address whether the force could grow in future, and did not say where the 15,000 number came from.

Source: Turkish Ministry of National Defence weekly media briefing, 12 November 2026 (Anadolu Agency).
