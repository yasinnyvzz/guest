NATO's industry and technology forum comes to Türkiye next week. The essentials, based on the Turkish defence ministry's 12 November 2026 briefing and the organiser's own material:

1. When and where

17-19 November 2026, at the Fuar İzmir exhibition grounds.

2. Who organises it

The NATO Communications and Information Agency (NCIA). On the host side, the Turkish General Staff coordinates.

3. How many firms

284 technology companies, according to Ankara. Thirty are Turkish.

4. What it is about

NCIA highlights AI and data-driven decision-making, cloud and edge computing, and cyber defence. The Turkish ministry frames it as NATO's technology needs and industry-Alliance cooperation.

5. What is not public

The exhibitor list and a national breakdown.

Sources: Turkish Ministry of National Defence (12 November 2026); NATO Communications and Information Agency.
