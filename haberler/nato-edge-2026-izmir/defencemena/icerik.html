Izmir will be the venue for NATO EDGE 2026 from 17 to 19 November, the Turkish defence ministry confirmed on Thursday. A total of 284 technology companies are due at the Izmir Fair Centre, 30 of them from Türkiye.

Who is running it

The Turkish General Staff is coordinating the three-day gathering, the ministry told reporters at its weekly briefing on 12 November 2026.

What it covers

Discussions will centre on the technology NATO needs as its security environment shifts, and on how defence manufacturers and the Alliance can work together.

Still to come

A list of exhibitors, a country-by-country breakdown and the detailed schedule have not been released.

Source: Turkish Ministry of National Defence weekly briefing, 12 November 2026.
