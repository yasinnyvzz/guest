The commander of the Turkish Naval Forces held contacts in Egypt on 4-9 November as part of Exercise Sea of Friendship-2026 (Dostluk Denizi-2026), while the Air Force commander is hosting the commander of the Kuwait Air Force in Türkiye on 11-14 November. The details were given at the Ministry of National Defence (MSB) weekly press briefing on 12 November 2026.

Egypt: Sea of Friendship-2026

According to the ministry's schedule, the navy commander visited the Turkish Embassy in Cairo on 6 November, met the commander of the Egyptian Navy on 7 November and attended the exercise's Distinguished Observer Day on 8 November.

Kuwait and Pakistan

The air force commander is hosting his Kuwaiti counterpart between 11 and 14 November. He is scheduled to pay an official visit to Pakistan on 16-19 November.

What comes next

The navy commander is expected to attend the Distinguished Observer Day of Exercise Blue Whale-2026 (Mavi Balina-2026) on 13 November. The content of the talks and the units taking part in the exercises were not given.

Source: Turkish Ministry of National Defence weekly press briefing, 12 November 2026.
