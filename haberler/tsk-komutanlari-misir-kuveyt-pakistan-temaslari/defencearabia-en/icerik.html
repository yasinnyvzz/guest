Türkiye's Naval Forces Commander, Admiral Ercüment Tatlıoğlu, travelled to Egypt for the Sea of Friendship-2026 naval exercise, which Egypt hosted in Alexandria from 4 to 9 November. Separately, the commander of the Kuwait Air Force arrived in Türkiye on 11 November.

Navy: Egypt

  • 6 November: visit to the Turkish Embassy in Cairo
  • 7 November: meeting with the commander of the Egyptian Navy
  • 8 November: Distinguished Observer Day of the exercise

Air force: Kuwait, then Pakistan

  • 11-14 November: Kuwait Air Force commander hosted in Türkiye
  • 16-19 November: Turkish Air Force commander's official visit to Pakistan

Background

Last year's Sea of Friendship exercise was held in Turkish waters; this year Egypt was the host. Ankara's defence ministry gave the dates above at its 12 November 2026 briefing and did not detail the talks.

Source: Turkish Ministry of National Defence weekly briefing, 12 November 2026.
