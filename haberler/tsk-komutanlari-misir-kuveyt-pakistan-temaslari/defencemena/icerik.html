Two Arab capitals featured on the Turkish military leadership's calendar within days of each other: Cairo through a joint naval exercise, Kuwait through a visit by its air force commander.

The Egyptian track

Türkiye's navy chief spent 4-9 November in Egypt for the Sea of Friendship-2026 exercise. While there he called at his country's embassy in Cairo (6 November), sat down with the head of the Egyptian Navy (7 November) and watched the Distinguished Observer Day (8 November).

The Kuwaiti track

From 11 to 14 November the commander of the Kuwait Air Force is the guest of his Turkish opposite number. Two days after that visit ends, the Turkish air chief leaves for an official trip to Pakistan lasting from 16 to 19 November.

What we don't know

The Turkish defence ministry listed these dates at its weekly briefing on 12 November 2026 without describing what was discussed or which ships took part in the drill.

Source: Turkish Ministry of National Defence weekly briefing, 12 November 2026.
