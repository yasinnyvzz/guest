# Roadmap: How can a submarine stay underwater for weeks?

Status: from the user's complete brief (2026-11-11), no roadmap gate · Profile: Balanced · Format: 76 s, 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps, silent

## 1. Concept
- **Logline:** One submarine is built and upgraded step by step; every problem of staying submerged (pressure, air, energy, noise, finding the way) gets a visible engineering answer, until the conceptual boat is revealed as the REIS class.
- **The turn:** Before: a hull that can dive but must come back up for air and charge. After: a boat that carries its own air management, AIP energy and quiet drive.
- **Ending:** the finished boat slides silently through dark water under a far surface; negative space holds the question "CAN IT STAY SUBMERGED FOR WEEKS?" and the Defence MENA wordmark.
- **How code shows it:** three.js, one hand-built Type 214-shaped hull (single model through the whole film: cylindrical body, faired bow, sail, cruciform stern planes, one 7-blade skewed propeller). Ghosted outer hull for cutaways; inner systems are simplified blocks, tanks, cells and flow lines, never invented internals.
- **Specificity test:** facts kept to the public and general: pressure hull vs light outer casing; crew air needs O2 supply and CO2 removal, separate from propulsion; diesel-electric boats run on batteries submerged; fuel-cell AIP turns stored hydrogen and stored oxygen into electricity and water, extending (not unlimiting) endurance; electric motor drives the propulsor; noise is managed by isolation; passive sonar listens, it does not see; REIS class = Type 214-derived (214TN). No numbers, no internal layouts.

**Tempo:** standard, ~7.5 s per chapter with 2-4 s beats inside each · 10 chapters
